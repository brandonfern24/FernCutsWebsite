The Craft Behind the Cut

I'm a 23-year-old barber with 5 years of experience, dedicated to delivering clean, sharp cuts at an affordable price.

My goal is to make every client look their best without breaking the bank — services start as low as $10–$30. Book with me and leave feeling confident every time.

I specialize in precision fades, trims, line-ups, beard grooming, and shape-ups. Whether you need a crisp fade, a fresh edge, or a clean beard finish, every detail matters.
